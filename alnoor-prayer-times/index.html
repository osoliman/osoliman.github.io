How times are set

Our goal is a schedule that is predictable, consistent, and faithful to the athan.

  • Iqama times are fixed and change only on Fridays, in 15-minute increments — so times never shift mid-week.
  • Fajr iqama is typically 15–30 minutes after the athan, leaving time for the sunnah before joining the jama’ah.
  • Isha iqama is kept as close to the athan as practical.
  • Athan times follow IslamicFinder for Mansfield, MA (ISNA calculation method).
  • Suggestions on these guidelines are always welcome through the feedback form below.
